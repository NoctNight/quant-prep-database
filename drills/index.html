Estimator and CLT Drills

Twelve problems in the style of a quant research phone screen, where a shared Python editor is open beside you. Every numeric answer here has been checked by simulation.

How to practise these

  1. Give yourself ten minutes per problem. That is roughly the real pace when three problems fill an hour and the interviewer dictates each one to you.
  2. Say the approach before you compute. Name the tool — conditioning, the CLT, law of total variance, an order statistic — and only then reach for numbers.
  3. Use the editor to verify, never to derive. "Let me sanity-check that with a simulation" is a strong move; "let me simulate and see" as a first instinct is a weak one.
  4. Read the solution only after you have written something down, even if it is wrong.
