### 1.4 Difficulty, style, what is rewarded, why people fail — PDT
- Engineering rounds are **LeetCode-easy/medium + a real C++ round + DP** at the intern/new-grad level; reviewers rate SWE among PDT's *easiest* interviews, in sharp contrast to QR (3.8/5). [FH]
- QR is described as "**harder than Citadel and Two Sigma** but good questions, doable with sufficient preparation" and elsewhere "much harder… with a quite different flavor" — open-ended mini-projects, stats depth, brainteasers, long written test. [FH WSO]
- Culture/fit weighs heavily (small firm, ~200 people); Blind posters report no feedback after rejection and a belief that a rejection limits re-applying. [FH]
- 2026 take-home explicitly forbids LLM use while allowing reference lookups — signals they grade reasoning, not completion. [FH]
- Founder Pete Muller's puzzle culture (office designed as a cryptic puzzle) is widely cited [PREP/press]; "puzzle"-style questions are corroborated first-hand only for the **QR** track ("brainteasers from the books").

### 1.5 Internship vs full-time — PDT
- **Intern/new-grad SWE:** campus/video stage → 1-hour engineer screen (LC easy/med, iterators) → loop of algorithm (DP) + C++ + experience. Fast (avg 14 days). No evidence of take-homes or brainteasers for interns. [FH]
- **Experienced SWE/QD:** recruiter → engineer phone screen (résumé + one coding problem) → multi-round panel day; sometimes a take-home (2026); can drag for months; questions can be domain-flavoured (SQL, derivatives) for trading-tech roles. [FH]
- **QR:** full-time PhD only; probability/stats/brainteasers, research talk, long written test; no QR internship advertised. [FH/OFF]
